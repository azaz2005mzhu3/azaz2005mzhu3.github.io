# Personal Website Proposal

## Who This Page Is For

This personal website is for anyone who wants to learn more about me as a person. It can be for my friends, classmates, professors, or people who meet me in the future and want to know more about my background and interests. I want the website to feel personal and give visitors a better idea of who I am outside of just school.

## The One Thing a Visitor Should Do

The main thing I want visitors to do is explore the website and get to know me better. After looking through the website, I hope they understand more about my personality, what I am interested in, where I come from, and some of the experiences that are important to me. I want the website to show more of my personal side instead of feeling like a resume.

## The Sections the Page Needs

The website will have several sections that introduce different parts of my life. I want to include an About Me section with some basic information about myself, an Interests section about the things I enjoy, and a Background section where I can share a short story about myself and some of my experiences. I also want to include personal photos throughout the website so that it feels more visual and personal.

## What Content I Have and What Is Missing

I already have personal photos and some basic information about myself that I can use for the website. I also know some of the interests and experiences that I want to talk about. What I still need to do is choose the best photos, organize my information, and write my background story in a way that is short but still interesting. I may also add more content as I build the website and decide what fits the page best.

## Two or Three Sites I Like and What I Like About Each

I like Apple's website because it is very simple, clean, and uses a lot of white space, so the page never feels too crowded. I also like Airbnb's website because it uses large photos and has a simple layout that makes it easy to look through different content. For my own website, I want to use some of these ideas while making it more personal. I want the overall design to be simple and organized, with light blue as the main color and personal photos as an important part of the website.
