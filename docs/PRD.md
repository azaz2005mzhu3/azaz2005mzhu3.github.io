# Personal Website PRD

## 1. Project Overview

This project is a personal website that introduces who I am beyond just school or a resume. The website will share some basic information about me, my background, my interests, and personal photos. The main goal is for visitors to learn more about me and leave the website feeling that I have an interesting and well-rounded life.

The website should feel soft, personal, and visually appealing. It should not feel too corporate or look like a professional resume website.

## 2. Target Audience

The website is for anyone who wants to learn more about me, including friends, classmates, professors, and people I may meet in the future.

Visitors should be able to quickly understand my background, what I am interested in, and some parts of my personality and life.

## 3. Main Goal

The main goal of the website is to help visitors get to know me better as a person.

After visiting the website, I want someone to think:

> "Alice has an interesting and well-rounded life."

The website should communicate this mostly through personal photos, short descriptions, and my interests rather than long paragraphs.

## 4. Website Structure

The website will be a single-page website.

The navigation will appear at the top of the page and will link to different sections of the same page. The navigation will not stay fixed when the visitor scrolls.

Navigation:

- About Me
- My Story
- What I Love
- Gallery

The overall page should be relatively short and easy to explore.

## 5. Hero Section

The first section visitors see will have a large personal photo.

It will include:

**Alice Zhu**

*Business Student & Student-Athlete*

The personal photo should be the main visual focus of the hero section. The design should be simple and have enough white space around the content.

## 6. About Me

The About Me section will give visitors basic information about me.

It will include:

- My name
- Where I am from
- My major / area of study
- My identity as a student-athlete

There will be a short and casual introduction followed by a few simple personal tags, such as:

`Business Student` `Student-Athlete` `Beijing`

The section should be short and easy to read.

## 7. My Story

The My Story section will briefly introduce my background.

The story will follow a simple order:

**My early background → Moving to the United States → My current college life and experience as a student-athlete**

This section does not need to tell my entire life story. It should only give visitors enough information to understand where I come from and some of the experiences that shaped me.

A natural, casual personal photo will appear next to the story.

## 8. What I Love

This section will introduce three of my main interests:

**Golf**

**Business**

**Baking**

This section should be very visual. Photos will be more important than text, with only short descriptions when necessary.

For Golf and Baking, I would like to use my own photos when possible. For Business, I can use a visually appealing image that matches the design of the website.

## 9. Gallery

The Gallery section will contain personal photos.

Instead of displaying all photos in a grid, the gallery will use a slideshow or carousel. Visitors will be able to move between photos using simple left and right controls.

The gallery should be easy to use and should not have complicated animations.

I already have some personal photos for the website, but I will continue choosing and adding photos while building the project.

## 10. Visual Style

The website should have a soft, personal, and aesthetic feeling.

The main color palette will be:

- Light blue
- Cream / beige
- White

Light blue will be the main accent color, while cream and white will help make the website feel warm and soft.

There should be plenty of open space so the website does not feel crowded.

## 11. Typography

The typography should feel elegant but still easy to read.

Headings can use a more elegant font to give the website personality, while paragraphs and navigation should use a simple and readable font.

Typography should support the aesthetic of the website without making it difficult to read.

## 12. Interactions and Animation

The website can include small animations and interactions.

Examples include:

- Light fade-in effects when sections enter the screen
- Small hover effects on images
- Smooth scrolling when navigation links are clicked
- Simple transitions in the photo carousel

Animations should be subtle and should not distract from the content.

## 13. Responsive Design

The website should work well on both desktop and mobile devices.

On smaller screens, images, text, navigation, and sections should automatically adjust to fit the screen.

The mobile version should still maintain the same soft and clean visual style as the desktop version.

## 14. Accessibility

The website only needs basic accessibility features.

Personal photos and important images should include alt text. Text should be easy to read against the background, and navigation and carousel controls should be easy to click and understand.

## 15. Technical Requirements

The website will be built using:

- HTML
- CSS
- Basic JavaScript

No additional frameworks are required.

HTML will organize the content, CSS will control the visual design and responsive layout, and JavaScript will be used for features such as the photo carousel and simple interactions.

## 16. Footer

The bottom of the website will have a very simple footer.

Example:

`© 2026 Alice Zhu`

There will not be a large call-to-action or contact section.

## 17. Content Tone

The writing should be casual and friendly.

It should sound like I am introducing myself naturally to someone instead of writing a professional biography. Paragraphs should stay short because the photos and visual design are an important part of telling the story.

## 18. What the Website Should Avoid

The website should not feel too corporate or look like an online resume.

It should avoid:

- Long blocks of text
- A corporate or overly professional design
- Resume-style sections
- Too many animations
- Bright or aggressive colors
- Crowded layouts
- Too much information on one screen

The final website should feel personal first and professional second.

## 19. Success Criteria

The project will be successful if:

1. Visitors can easily understand who I am.
2. Visitors can learn about my background and interests without reading a lot of text.
3. My personal photos are an important part of the experience.
4. The website feels soft, personal, and visually appealing.
5. The website works well on both desktop and mobile.
6. Visitors leave with the impression that I have an interesting and well-rounded life.
